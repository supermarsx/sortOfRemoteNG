sortOfRemoteNG keeps saved connections, remote sessions, and administration tools together. Start with one computer, organize a larger library with folders and tags, and open the tools you need in tabs or detached windows.

## A few useful distinctions

<div class="feature-grid">
  <div class="feature-card">
    <strong>Save once, connect again</strong>
    <p>Keep addresses and connection settings in your library, organized with folders, tags, and recognizable names.</p>
  </div>
  <div class="feature-card">
    <strong>Sessions are your open work</strong>
    <p>Open saved connections in tabs or detached windows. Closing a session does not delete its saved connection.</p>
  </div>
  <div class="feature-card">
    <strong>Protect saved data deliberately</strong>
    <p>Review application-file protection, individual database protection, and export options separately. Keep recovery material somewhere safe.</p>
  </div>
  <div class="feature-card">
    <strong>Check who you connect to</strong>
    <p>Review host keys and certificates in the Trust Center. Verify an unexpected identity change before accepting it.</p>
  </div>
</div>
